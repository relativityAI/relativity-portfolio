# Case Studies (short)

Source: podcast recorded ~January 2026. Numbers are approximate and likely stale. Verify first.
These are hindsight winners, not recommendations. The same signals also fail.

## Scan and themes
- **Auto ancillaries.** ~16 names in the 20% list. Lumax Auto, SJS, Fiem, Shriram Pistons together. Lumax went ~500-570 to ~1,500. Lesson: many names from one industry = something is starting.
- **Gold-loan financiers.** CSB Bank, Fedfina, IIFL, Manappuram all near highs. Fedfina ranged ~4 weeks, then hit a 52-week high (~125-130) while the index fell. Lesson: prep before the trigger.
- **KOSPI vs Nifty.** KOSPI ~+90% in a year. Nifty sideways. Two memory-chip names dominate KOSPI. Lesson: compare indices too.

## Long-base breakouts
- **BSE.** Downtrend, then ~1 year base. May 2021: volume spike, base breakout, close above 40-week MA. Multibagger.
- **Garware Hi-Tech Films.** 3-4 week pause near all-time high, then breakout. ~5x.
- **HBL Engineering.** Base Sep 2022 to mid-2023. Breakout with volume spikes.
- **RVNL.** Sep 2022 breakout. Then repeated 80-100% legs, each followed by a base. Every base was an entry.
- **Mahindra & Mahindra.** Long-term breakout ~900 in 2022. ~3,600 in the recording.
- **Ashok Leyland.** Breakout mid/late 2025. ~12 weeks near the high first. Then ~120-140 to ~185-190.

## IPO bases
- **Eternal (Zomato).** 30-week MA turned up ~29 May 2023. RS and volume rose. One-year high in July 2023, far below the IPO high. Trend ran to ~Jan 2025.
- **CAMS.** IPO, uptrend, top Aug 2021, 1-1.5 year base, breakout ~Oct-Nov 2023.

## Tops
- **Tejas Networks.** Orders fell. Dipped below the 40-week MA ~Feb 2024 with no follow-through. Real breakdown came later. Waiting for follow-through avoided an early exit.
- **IRCTC, IEX, DMart.** All topped ~18 Oct 2021. Vertical rise, then one-bar reversal.
- **Dixon, Trent.** Slow tops. Growth faded. No climax.
- **Gensol.** Fraud. Elevator fall.

## Exits
- **Tata Elxsi.** Bought ~900. Called "overvalued" for years. Trailing stop kept the holder in. Chart gave the exit.
- **Apar.** Weekly MA held through a sharp dip. The speaker admits he exited early, well before the peak.

## Mistake
- **Averaging down (DHFL).** A relative's average cost fell from ~350-400 to ~70-75. Exit ~40-45. About 70% of the portfolio was in it.
