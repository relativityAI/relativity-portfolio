# Chart Details

Read this for the "why" behind the rules.

## Trend theory lineage
- **Dow Theory** (1890s): uptrend = higher highs and lows. Downtrend = lower highs and lows.
- **Stage analysis** (Weinstein) extends Dow.
- **Darvas boxes**: join highs and lows. Consolidations stack as boxes in an uptrend.
- A chart reflects a business. Uptrend, then downtrend, then sideways is a business fading.

## Why stage 2 starts (earnings logic)
1. A dull, forgotten stock bases for years.
2. A positive surprise arrives. EPS beats estimates.
3. Analysts revise estimates up. Target prices rise.
4. Institutions read that research and buy. Price and volume rise.
5. EPS momentum builds. The trend runs.

- After a long drawdown, the first revisions often start a 1.5-2 year run.
- Apar Industries: sideways 2011-19. From 2020, about 12 straight quarters of upward revisions.

## Why stage 3/4 starts
- First slow quarter from a fast grower (Peter Lynch's warning).
- Base effect: a huge quarter last year makes this year look like a collapse.
- Valuation far ahead of earnings.
- External shock.

## Overhead supply
- Trapped buyers sell when price returns to their cost.
- A 1+ year base lets them leave. Less supply above.
- No base means the stock needs huge volume to power through.
- "I'll sell when it gets back to my price" is how that supply forms.

## Long base vs short base
- Limited float: smart money cannot buy all at once. Stock runs, pauses, runs again.
- Sideways after a run means supply is not coming. The market waits for results.
- Long-base breakouts often signal a business transition. Many multibaggers start here.

## IPO bases
- IPO high = reference level.
- Fresh IPO high breakout: meaningful.
- IPO high 3-5+ years old: weak signal. Use to add to winners only.
- Do not wait for the IPO high if the stock already doubled from lows. A 20% near-high scan catches it earlier.

## Weekly MA in practice
- Apar fell about 1,800 to 1,400 in weeks (Jan 2023). It never broke the 40-week MA. Then it ran to about 10,000.
- Daily-chart investors got shaken out. The weekly MA said "trend intact".
- Most multibaggers show higher highs and higher lows above the 40-week MA.

## Tops in detail
- **Slow top:** an internal story fades (order book, growth). Time to exit.
- **Climax top:** crowded, expensive stock. One shock reverses it in a day.
- **Fraud:** elevator down. Fundamentals, not charts, protect you.
- Good business and good stock are different. Entry price matters.

## Time correction and cycles
- Great companies can go sideways 3-5 years (SRF, Kotak Bank, HDFC Bank, HUL, Nestle).
- Capital sitting still has an opportunity cost.
- Leadership rotates about every 5 years (data since 1991).
- Shocks reshuffle themes. Pharma and IT led after COVID. Then railways, defence, solar. Then gold and metals (as of the recording).
- In bubbles, ride with a trailing stop. The first exit signal is the burst.

## Scan behaviour
- Pick strong stocks first. Sector second. By the time a sector is in the news, leaders have moved.
- Analogy: if 5 of the league's top 10 scorers play for one club, that club is strong.
- A stock holding near highs in a weak market is the best RS signal.
